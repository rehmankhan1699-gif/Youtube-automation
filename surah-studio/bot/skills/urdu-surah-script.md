# Urdu Surah crux script (7–8 minutes)

Use this when writing narration for `generate_voiceover` and `save_package`.

## Target length

- Spoken Urdu ≈ **1000–1200 words** (about 7–8 minutes at calm pace).
- Structure into timed sections the motion brief can mirror.

## Structure

1. **Hook (0:00–0:40)** — Surah name, one emotional/practical promise.
2. **Context (0:40–1:30)** — Meccan/Medinan, why it was revealed (high level).
3. **Crux themes (1:30–6:00)** — 3–5 core teachings with short examples.
4. **Living lesson (6:00–7:20)** — how a viewer applies this today.
5. **Close (7:20–8:00)** — dua-style soft close + CTA to next Surah / subscribe.

## Style

- Warm, clear Pakistani/standard Urdu. Avoid heavy Persianized prose.
- Do not invent ayah numbers or hadith. Prefer paraphrase of well-known meanings.
- No clickbait claims ("secret Surah doctors hate").
- Keep sentences speakable for TTS (avoid dense nested clauses).
