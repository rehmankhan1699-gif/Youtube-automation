# YouTube + Instagram packaging

Use keyword research from `research_keywords` and times from `research_best_times`.

## YouTube (long form)

- Title ≤ 70 chars when possible; lead with Surah name + "خلاصہ" / "Explained in Urdu".
- Description: first 2 lines sell the crux; then timestamps; then soft CTA; then hashtags.
- Tags: mix Urdu transliteration + English Islamic terms from the keyword pack.
- Schedule into the proposed long-form slot (Thu/Fri evening Asia/Karachi by default).

## Instagram

- Caption: hook line first, then 3–5 short lines, CTA to full YouTube video.
- 8–15 hashtags from the pack; no spam walls of 30+.
- Clips: 30–60s, vertical, first 1–2 seconds = text hook.
- Schedule Reels into evening + Fajr windows from `research_best_times`.

## Clips

Produce at least 3 clips with `startSec`/`endSec` from the motion brief, each with a hook line and platform.
